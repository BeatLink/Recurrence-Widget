<recurrence-input>

A form field for editing iCalendar recurrence rules, built on rrule.js. Each example below shows the value the form would submit and the next few dates it produces, counted from now; times are local clock times (time-mode="floating"). Source and usage.

A new rule

Starts empty, meaning the item does not repeat. This uses the default, features="standard".

Weekly on chosen days

Every two weeks on Monday and Wednesday, ten times.

A weekday of the month

The last Friday of every month, until the end of next year.

The last weekday of the month

Every weekday, then only the last of them each month (BYSETPOS=-1).

Several times a day

Weekdays at 09:00 and 17:00; add or remove times with the buttons.

All-day, with skipped dates

all-day writes dates without times, and value-format="ical" adds EXDATE and RDATE lines for dates to skip or add.

The simple form

features="simple" shows one time, one day pattern and no extras. The second time and the weekday filter in this rule are hidden but kept, and the description still includes them.

Kept as written

Parts the form cannot edit, such as X- names or an RSCALE calendar, are kept exactly as written. features="full" adds More options, which edits every standard part directly.

A time of day, and a yearly date

The second Sunday of March at 02:00, every year.

Styled by the page

The controls are ordinary page elements with recurrence-input__* classes, so this card restyles them with plain CSS; weekday-format="narrow" shortens the day names.

Paste a rule

Load any rule, with or without the RRULE: prefix. Text that is not a rule makes the form invalid.
