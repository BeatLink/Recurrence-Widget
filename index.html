<recurrence-input>

A form field for editing iCalendar recurrence rules, built on rrule.js. Each example below shows the value the form would submit and the next few dates the rule produces, counted from today. Source and usage.

A new rule

Starts empty, meaning the item does not repeat.

Weekly on chosen days

Every two weeks on Monday and Wednesday, ten times.

A weekday of the month

The last Friday of every month, until the end of next year.

Parts the form does not show

BYMONTHDAY=15 has no control, but it is kept when you change the interval or the end.

Paste a rule

Load any rule, with or without the RRULE: prefix. Text that is not a rule makes the form invalid.
